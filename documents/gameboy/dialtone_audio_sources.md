# Dialtone Audio Sources

Dialtone now uses tiny in-ROM Game Boy PSG arrangements and hand-tuned SFX instead of PCM playback. The music cues were compacted for ROM size and shaped around these royalty-free source tracks:

- `Rain Loop`:
  - `Mysterious, Futuristic 8-bit Music Loop` by Frenchyboy, CC0
  - https://opengameart.org/content/mysterious-futuristic-8-bit-music-loop
- `Vending Dreams`:
  - `My Street` by congusbongus, CC0
  - https://opengameart.org/content/my-street
- `Rooftop Set`:
  - `Society In Ruins` by Spring Spring, CC0 available on the page
  - https://opengameart.org/content/society-in-ruins

Reference SFX sources used to guide the in-game menu, door, and reward sound palette:

- `NES 8-bit sound effects` by shiru8bit, CC0/CC-BY 3.0 choice
  - https://opengameart.org/content/nes-8-bit-sound-effects
- `512 Sound Effects (8-bit style)` by SubspaceAudio, CC0
  - https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style
