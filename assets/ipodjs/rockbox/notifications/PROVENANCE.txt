# iOS 5 notification surfaces

These are rendered-source assets from original iOS 5-era captures, not
replacement theme artwork.

## Notification Center

Source page:
https://theapplewiki.com/wiki/File:Notifications_Center_in_ios_5.png

Original capture:
https://theapplewiki.com/images/applewiki/d/dd/Notifications_Center_in_ios_5.png

Original SHA-256:
`7fc5cbc545fee928fbcaff802a41eaf8e93c2bd6f5026a2084d0f8c96b766f31`

The 640x960 Retina capture was reduced to its 320px logical rendering with a
box filter. Exact 32x32 linen, 32x24 section-header, and 22x22 close-button
regions were then cropped. No pixels were painted.

## Notification banner

Source page:
https://www.businessinsider.com/iphone-notifications-2011-6

Original capture:
https://i.insider.com/4ded15a14bd7c8195d2d0000

Original SHA-256:
`ecd44cee567641787e5109b6ac9781062b7bf4e02e500a00bba0da3612222eed`

The photographed banner plane was perspective-corrected. A clean vertical
slice of Apple's rendered BannerGradient surface was extended to 320x42.
Dynamic notification text and source icons are rendered by Rockbox over it.

All shipped BMPs are uncompressed RGB24. The two source-icon BMPs use
Rockbox's top-left magenta color key for transparent pixels; their retained
edge pixels are preblended against the iOS 5 app-header tone:

- `achievement-icon.22x22x24.bmp` — a Lanczos reduction of the packaged
  32x32 Xbox 360 sphere source icon with a soft circular alpha mask, used so
  Notification Center draws a complete mark without its original square
  background;
  `ffa8ffaf60d5fcb411219b018851cb407476ed741684df2ddcd8a302b0ea6d84`
- `sitekick-icon.22x22x24.bmp` — a Lanczos reduction of Sitekick's packaged
  YTV source icon for the same fixed app-header slot, converted from its
  original alpha channel to the native color key;
  `c6fc664d15ddb329e97e4ae50dfab33997e63a8c5caf57f3bd59f0164fc03e6f`
- `notification-banner.ios5.320x42x24.bmp` — `9fee12e586ba6beb8543c62af70592148fddc8736f72a198a3a998a05c5c075b`
- `notification-center-linen.ios5.32x32x24.bmp` — `4d7152031e9baa894b15c0cce4fbc261115d0bf719aa8d0df0541466c3da1b2e`
- `notification-close.ios5.22x22x24.bmp` — `9931d3e650324382adedf58bb1f365d61392e222b0097c9ff2f43daeb1f62f43`
- `notification-section.ios5.32x24x24.bmp` — `7518a4f61bf53a47030a018dd4fc06e77c21db1cd4f80cef102ed1b870cdfa9a`
