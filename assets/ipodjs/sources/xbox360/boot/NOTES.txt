Xbox 360 2005 console boot source
=================================

Selected sequence:
  dark horizon -> metallic sphere -> green X -> Xbox 360 wordmark

This is the original 2005 console startup, not the later bright-white
ribbon-and-sphere Xbox 360 brand/game mnemonic.

Captured source:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=92coix3E_vY
  Title: Original Xbox 360 Startup (1080p)
  Uploader: Patexi
  Duration: 11.454 seconds
  Video: 1920x1080, 30000/1001 fps, AV1
  Audio: 48000 Hz stereo Opus

Authoritative creator references:
  https://vimeo.com/225005083
  https://www.audiobrain.com/xbox-360-press-release
  https://static1.squarespace.com/static/55916bdce4b06f59a48a09f8/t/5c743e31971a1821727b439c/1551121969398/Microsoft_Official%2BAudiobrain_XBox_360%2BPress_Release.pdf

Audiobrain states that it worked with Microsoft on the Xbox 360 startup,
sonic signature, user-interface sounds, and product sonification. Its public
Vimeo master is access-blocked from the build environment, so the checked-in
video is a clean console-boot capture. The separate .opus file is a stream
copy of that capture's audio; it has not been synthesized or recreated.
