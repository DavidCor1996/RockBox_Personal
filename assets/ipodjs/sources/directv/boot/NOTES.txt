DIRECTV receiver boot screen
============================

Source recording of a DIRECTV receiver powering up, used only to cut the
start-up animation the Live TV launcher plays while it sets up playback.
The recording is silent, so the ident is picture only.

tools/prepare_directv_boot_assets.sh cuts the segment, centre-cuts it to
320x240 the same way Live TV treats its own channels, and packs the frames
as RGB565 deltas into
assets/ipodjs/rockbox/livetv/boot/boot-320x240.nfr, which buildzip.pl
copies to .rockbox/ipodjs/livetv/boot on the iPod.

Personal offline build only. No DIRECTV service, account, or stream is
involved; the Live TV channels themselves are the user's own files.
