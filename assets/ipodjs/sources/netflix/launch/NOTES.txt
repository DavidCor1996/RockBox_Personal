Netflix modern ident source
===========================

Official Netflix upload:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GV3HUDMQ-F8
  Title: Netflix New Logo Animation 2019
  Publisher/channel: Netflix

Netflix describes this as its revamped dark-background ident, with the red N
breaking into a spectrum of colors while retaining the Netflix sound:
  https://about.netflix.com/en/news/your-new-netflix-ident-animation-cue-netflix-sound

The checked-in source and all derived frames/audio are for this personal,
offline Rockbox build only.
