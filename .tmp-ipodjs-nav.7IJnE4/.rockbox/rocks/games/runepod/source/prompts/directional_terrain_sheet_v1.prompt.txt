Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: game asset reference sheet for a 320x240 iPod Classic Rockbox RPG
plugin

directional player sprites and terrain/background tiles. Row 1 should show the

water, village wall/roof, and cave ground.

Style:
Match the existing RunePod low-poly-inspired 2D fantasy look: three-quarter
top-down camera, compact silhouettes, hand-painted texture, muted natural
palette, dark readable outline, upper-left lighting, no photorealism.

Layout:
Clean grid/contact sheet with generous padding, no labels and no text.

Background:
Perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for later removal. The
background must be one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor
plane, lighting variation, or reflections. Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the
sprites or tiles.

Constraints:
No RuneScape, no OSRS, no copyrighted assets, no copied skill icons, no
recognizable proprietary names or maps, no watermark, no text.
