SM64 upstream
=============

The engine source is based on the `dos` branch of
`fgsfdsfgs/sm64-port`, pinned at commit
`da5b744d6b120cbdedea5686f4076508bc8902bb`.

No ROM or extracted game asset is committed or included in `rockbox.zip`.
Run `tools/sm64_prepare_assets.py` with an owned US v1.0 ROM to regenerate
the ignored build inputs. The accepted SHA-1 is
`9bef1128717f958171a4afac3ed78ee2bb4e86ce`.

Rockbox-specific changes are limited to the platform frontend, software
renderer integration, save I/O, compatibility fixes, and build wiring.
