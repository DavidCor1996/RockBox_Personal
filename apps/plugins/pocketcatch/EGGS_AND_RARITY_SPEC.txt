PocketCatch Eggs and Rarity
===========================

Spawn rarity
------------

Wild spawn tables are weighted by route. Common Pokemon use the largest
weights, uncommon Pokemon use mid weights, and rare route-table species use
small weights. The first pass uses representative Kanto encounter data from
the original route identity:

- Route 1: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_Route_1
  Pidgey and Rattata are common, Spearow and Nidoran are uncommon.
- Viridian Forest: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Viridian_Forest
  Caterpie and Weedle lines dominate, Pikachu is rare.
- Route 24: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_Route_24
  Route 24 and Route 25 grass encounters favor Oddish/Bellsprout-style common
  species, with Abra and Pikachu treated as rare.

Spawn pacing is intentionally faster than before: visible spawn cap is 7,
inactive slots roll every completed step, respawn delay is 3-9 step ticks, and
lifetimes are 64-150 step ticks.

Egg acquisition
---------------

PokeStops can award eggs after a successful spin.

- Drop chance: 24% when at least one egg slot is free.
- Slots: 3 egg slots.
- Full inventory: no egg is awarded, but normal Poke Ball and coin rewards
  still apply.
- New eggs baseline current music runtime immediately, so music played before
  acquisition does not progress that egg.

Egg rarity and hatch time
-------------------------

Egg rarity is based on a species rarity table inspired by Pokemon encounter
scarcity and special availability.

- Common: 8 minutes of music.
- Uncommon: 12 minutes of music.
- Rare: 18 minutes of music.
- Epic: 24 minutes of music.

Rare examples include Pikachu, Abra, Clefairy, starters, Eevee, and Pichu.
Epic examples include Chansey, Scyther, and Misdreavus.

Incubation progress
-------------------

Eggs progress from Rockbox playback runtime, not walking.

- Runtime source: audio_current_track() path hash plus elapsed milliseconds.
- Sidecar file: .rockbox/rocks/apps/pocketcatch/egg_runtime.dat.
- If music is playing outside PocketCatch, reopening PocketCatch while that
  track is still playing credits the elapsed delta since the last stored
  observation.
- While PocketCatch is open, progress updates about once per second.
- Paused or stopped playback does not progress eggs.
- Each observed delta applies to all active egg slots.

When an egg hatches, the Pokemon is added to the bag, the family receives 10
candy, and the egg slot is cleared.
